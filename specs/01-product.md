# 01 — The product

## In one sentence

Tabletop Twin is a small physical model of one person's next 90 days of money
that sits on their table and can be pushed, pulled and questioned by hand.

The person in the model is Juno Reyes, a freelance illustrator. Her income
arrives late and unevenly. The model already contains several weeks of her
history, so the table has something to show the first time it is opened.

## What the model shows

| You see | It means |
| --- | --- |
| A river running across the table | Her cash over the next 90 days. The width is the typical forecast; the banks show a likely good and a likely bad case. |
| Stones in the river | A bill on the day it is due. A stone below the water line is a bill the forecast cannot cover. |
| A boat in fog | An invoice that is late. The fog shows how likely it is to be later still. |
| A lake beside the river | Money set aside as savings. |
| A garden | How reliable the twin's past predictions turned out to be. |
| An orb | The twin itself. It looks at and points to whatever it is talking about. |

The message is the shape. Words are a caption for the shape, never a substitute
for it.

## What a person does with it

- Turn the time wheel to move through the 90 days.
- Pull a ribbon to ask "what if this bill moved?"
- Pull a subscription pipe to ask "what is this subscription costing me?"
- Hold a pinch for one second to approve a payment; reject it with a panel or
  with voice.
- Ask a question, out loud or in text, and hear an answer that points at the
  table.

Every one of those is a request. The table does not change the world on its own.

## What it is not

One person, one kind of problem, one 90-day horizon. No bank connection, no
investment advice, no crypto, no multiple players, no avatars of real people.

## What "finished" means

- Every figure on the table can be traced back to a calculation.
- Every claim the twin makes is saved and can be checked later.
- Every refusal can be explained by naming the rule that refused.
- Anything said in one channel — speech, caption, shape — is available in
  another.
